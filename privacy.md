# Real Life OS Privacy Policy
**Effective date:** October 16, 2026  
**Who we are:** Real Life OS is operated by Alex Nielsen ("we," "us").
**Contact:** alexn963@gmail.com

Real Life OS helps you plan and do real things with people you choose.

## What we store

Your account (email, display name) and the things you create: Spaces, posts, comments, plans, and your own statements about your life. When you type an ask, we keep it exactly as you typed it, visible only to you.

### Optional calendar connection

Calendar is an **optional** connection. We only read it after you allow it in the app (and in the system permission dialog). Declining leaves the rest of the app usable.

When you allow calendar access, we read:

- event times (start and end, including all-day),
- event titles,
- location text on the event, and
- the **number** of invitees (attendee count),

for events in a window of **12 months back plus all future events**.

We **never** read:

- event notes, or
- attendee names or email addresses.

Before we store an event, we drop it entirely if its title or place is sensitive (for example medical, therapy, pharmacy, school, childcare, worship, or a title that names the thing itself, such as a recovery meeting). Location text for those kinds of places is also dropped and never stored. Person names are stripped from remaining titles before anything is sent to an AI provider.

You can disconnect calendar in the app at any time. Disconnecting, or losing the system permission, deletes what we read from your calendar and anything we derived from it.

### Optional location while you use the app

Location is optional. We only use it if you allow it, and **only while the app is open**. We do not import any past location history.

When you allow it, we match where you are to a **public place** (for example a venue) using **Google** as a service provider that receives your coordinates for that match, or to a **neighborhood** when you are not at a venue. We never store or show a street address.

We drop sensitive places and never store them: medical, therapy, pharmacy, school, childcare, worship, and homes.

Precise coordinates are kept for at most **14 days**, then reduced to counts (area, weekday, and date) kept up to **12 months**.

Friends and other people never see your location or where you have been. Only you do.

Turning location off, or revoking the system permission, deletes your location data and anything built from it. Deleting something we noticed about places you go also deletes the underlying location data that supported it.

### Features we may add later (not collected under this version)

Photo library access may be added later as an **optional** connection, asked for on its own screen when that feature needs it. We do **not** collect photo library data under this version of the policy. Before any build that asks for photos, we will update this policy and the App Privacy answers, and link the updated copy in the app.

## Web replies without an account

If you open a link from someone and don't have an account, we store the name you type and whether you're down or can't, only for that one intention. No location from that page. The link expires and the sender can turn it off.

## What we do not collect today

Contacts, push tokens, or photo library contents. Calendar and location while the app is open are only used if you opt in, as described above.

## Who can see what

Space content is visible only to that Space's members. Content addressed to one relationship is visible only to the two of you. Personal content, your Life Model, and your location data are visible only to you. Plan executions are visible only to the plan's owner and the people it was addressed to.

## Analytics

Product analytics record event names and non-content properties (such as counts and ids), never what you wrote.

## AI

When AI features are on, we may send a structured digest of your own accessible data to an AI provider (**Anthropic**, or **OpenAI** if that provider is configured) so the app can help you find and plan real things. The digest is built only from what you have access to. It is not used to track you across other apps, and other users do not see it. You can delete inferred items about you in the app.

Phase 0 of the product may ship before any external AI call is live; the same rules apply as soon as AI is enabled.

## Booking and third parties

Real Life OS never books, pays, or sends messages for you. Booking links open the venue's or ticket seller's own site.

When you ask the app to find places or events, we may send a city or a place query (without your user id and without Space contents) to providers such as Google Places or Ticketmaster so we can return real options. Separately, if you allowed location while using the app, **Google** may receive coordinates solely to match you to a public place, as described above. Those providers process the request under their own terms.

## Deletion

You can delete your account in the app at any time (Settings → Delete account). Deletion removes your account, profile, consent log, Life Model data, content you solely own, and media you uploaded, as described in the product's account-deletion policy. Spaces you owned pass to another member when possible; experiences shared with others stay for them without your participant row.
